Is BlueCrest Recovery Center worth it?

Woodland Park, NJ · Passaic County

1.6/5

Short answer: it depends on whether you need what it is strongest at. BlueCrest Recovery Center scores highest on program depth and lowest on clinical clarity.

The strongest thing about it

Program depth

5.0

The only center in this group offering the entire ladder from medical detox through residential to outpatient and aftercare under one provider.

The weakest

Clinical clarity

4.0

The model is stated clearly as integrating clinical, spiritual and holistic methods with 12-step principles. Session-level scheduling detail is thinner.

Worth it for

  • Someone who may need detox and wants to step down through levels without changing providers
  • People who want or are open to a 12-step framework
  • Anyone who values a stack of third-party accreditations

Not worth it for

  • People on Medicaid or Medicare, which the center states it does not accept
  • Anyone specifically seeking a non-12-step approach
  • People whose primary problem is mental health rather than substance use

Before you decide

Ask these four. The answers move this from a ranking to a decision.

  1. What is your state license number and expiration date?
  2. How long is the typical detox stay, and what determines the move to residential?
  3. How central is the 12-step component, and what is offered to someone who does not want it?
  4. Since Medicaid and Medicare are not accepted, what is the self-pay rate per level of care?

*Provider-reported information, current as of August 2026; confirm before admission. Inclusion does not imply endorsement. Details can change.
