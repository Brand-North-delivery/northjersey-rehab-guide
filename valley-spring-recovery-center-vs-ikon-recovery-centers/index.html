Valley Spring Recovery Center vs IKON Recovery Centers

Both serve Northern New Jersey. Valley Spring Recovery Center is in Norwood, NJ, IKON Recovery Centers in Saddle Brook, NJ. They differ most in what happens when someone needs more than an outpatient schedule.

Choose one over the other

Valley Spring Recovery Center if…

  • Someone whose primary problem is mental health rather than substance use, since the standalone mental health license means no substance use diagnosis is required
  • People who cannot stop working and need an evening intensive outpatient schedule
  • First responders looking for a program built for them

IKON Recovery Centers if…

  • People who want a specialty track — executive, first responder or LGBTQ-focused
  • Anyone who wants to verify a license number before calling
  • People already stabilized who do not need withdrawal management

Where each one is thin

Valley Spring Recovery Center

Medical director and psychiatric prescribing are named as part of the program. There is no on-site detox, so withdrawal management is arranged elsewhere — ask how that handoff is handled.

IKON Recovery Centers

Medication-assisted treatment and Vivitrol are offered, but the site states detox is not provided in house and is arranged through partners.
