Why people look elsewhere

Taken from North Jersey Recovery Center’s own published materials, these are the situations it is least suited to.

  • People on Medicaid
  • Anyone who needs on-site detox or residential care
  • People who want to see accreditation before making contact — none is published

Five alternatives

Valley Spring Recovery Center

Norwood, NJ · Bergen County

3.2/5

Valley Spring Recovery Center holds dual New Jersey licenses for substance use and mental health treatment, publishes its program schedules, and runs an evening intensive outpatient track built around people who are still working.

Consider it instead if: someone whose primary problem is mental health rather than substance use, since the standalone mental health license means no substance use diagnosis is required.

Full review →

BlueCrest Recovery Center

Woodland Park, NJ · Passaic County

1.6/5

The most complete ladder in this group, running from medical detox through residential and down to outpatient, with a stated 12-step and holistic model.

Consider it instead if: someone who may need detox and wants to step down through levels without changing providers.

Full review →

IKON Recovery Centers

Saddle Brook, NJ · Bergen County

1.5/5

A broad outpatient offering with several named specialty tracks and a published state license number, but detox is arranged through partners rather than delivered on site.

Consider it instead if: people who want a specialty track — executive, first responder or LGBTQ-focused.

Full review →

ChoicePoint

Fair Lawn, NJ · Bergen County

1.0/5

An outpatient program with unusually specific published detail on medication-assisted treatment, and one of the few in this group stating it accepts Medicare and Medicaid.

Consider it instead if: people on Medicare or Medicaid, who have very few options in this group.

Full review →

Boca Recovery Center — Englewood

Englewood, NJ · Bergen County

0.5/5

The Northern New Jersey site of a multi-state operator, listing medical detox, mental health services and specialized trauma and eating disorder therapy alongside outpatient care.

Consider it instead if: people who need trauma or eating disorder treatment alongside substance use care.
