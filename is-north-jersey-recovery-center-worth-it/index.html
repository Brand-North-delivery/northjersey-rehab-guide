Is North Jersey Recovery Center worth it?

Fair Lawn, NJ · Bergen County

0.8/5

Short answer: it depends on whether you need what it is strongest at. North Jersey Recovery Center scores highest on clinical clarity and lowest on continuing care.

The strongest thing about it

Clinical clarity

4.3

The separation of the mental health and substance use tracks is stated explicitly, and the mental health conditions treated are listed by name.

The weakest

Continuing care

3.8

Less publicly documented than the rest of the offering.

Worth it for

  • People who need an evening schedule and want a mental health track that is genuinely separate
  • Anyone stepping down from a higher level of care elsewhere
  • Commercial PPO and POS plan holders

Not worth it for

  • People on Medicaid
  • Anyone who needs on-site detox or residential care
  • People who want to see accreditation before making contact — none is published

Before you decide

Ask these four. The answers move this from a ranking to a decision.

  1. What is your state license number and expiration date, and are you accredited by CARF or The Joint Commission?
  2. Does the mental health track have its own license, or does admission require a substance use diagnosis?
  3. What are the evening IOP hours?
  4. What does aftercare look like once the program ends?

*Provider-reported information, current as of August 2026; confirm before admission. Inclusion does not imply endorsement. Details can change.
